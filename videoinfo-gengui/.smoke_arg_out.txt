=== 模拟实际 yt-dlp 命令 (Peppa Pig 单视频, 启用章节切分) ===

主模板 + chapter: 前缀模板:
  -o 'Peppa Pig - Official Channel/SUMMER FUN at the Water Park.%(ext)s'
  -o 'chapter:Peppa Pig - Official Channel/SUMMER FUN at the Water Park/chapters/%(section_number)02d - %(section_title)s/%(section_number)02d - %(section_title)s.%(ext)s'

完整命令片段 (URL 之前部分):
  yt-dlp \
      --no-playlist \
      -o \
      Peppa Pig - Official Channel/SUMMER FUN at the Water Park.%(ext)s \
      -o \
      chapter:Peppa Pig - Official Channel/SUMMER FUN at the Water Park/chapters/%(section_number)02d - %(section_title)s/%(section_number)02d - %(section_title)s.%(ext)s \
      --ffmpeg-location \
      D:/ffmpeg \
      --cookies-from-browser \
      chrome \
      --remote-components \
      ejs:github \
      -S \
      vcodec:h264,ext:mp4:m4a \
      --merge-output-format \
      mp4 \
      --write-subs \
      --write-auto-subs \
      --sub-format \
      json3 \
      --sub-langs \
      en.*,zh-Hans.* \
      --write-thumbnail \
      --convert-thumbnails \
      jpg \
      --write-info-json \
      --sponsorblock-remove \
      sponsor,intro,outro,interaction \
      --split-chapters \
      --no-keep-video \
      --newline \
      URL

预期产出:
  Peppa Pig - Official Channel/
  ├── SUMMER FUN at the Water Park.mp4  (主视频, --no-keep-video 后删除)
  ├── SUMMER FUN at the Water Park.en.json3  (主字幕, 真实标题)
  ├── SUMMER FUN at the Water Park.info.json  (主信息, 真实标题)
  ├── SUMMER FUN at the Water Park.jpg  (主缩略图, 真实标题)
  ├── SUMMER FUN at the Water Park.asr.wav  (ASR 音频)
  └── chapters/
      ├── 01 - Intro/
      │   ├── 01 - Intro.mp4  (章节视频, yt-dlp 切分后)
      │   ├── 01 - Intro.info.json  (章节信息, yt-dlp 写入)
      │   ├── 01 - Intro.en.json3  (切片字幕, 我们的代码生成)
      │   └── 01 - Intro.jpg  (章节封面, ffmpeg 抽帧)
      ├── 02 - Pool Time/...
      └── 03 - End/...
